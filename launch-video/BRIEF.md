# AI Accountant — 20s Launch Film ("Month-end, deleted.")

16:9, 1920×1080, 30fps, exactly 20.0 seconds. Music-only (no voiceover) — X autoplays muted,
so every beat must read as type and motion alone; the score adds punch when unmuted.

## Look

- Canvas: pure black `#000000`. Type: white `#FFFFFF`, secondary grey `#666666`.
- ONE accent: electric indigo `#314DD0` — reserved for the "AI moments" (the star, check marks,
  the scan line, the CTA). Never more than one accent element fighting per frame.
- Type: Helvetica Neue / Inter Tight fallback, Bold for slams, Medium for UI, letter-spacing -0.04em,
  tight leading. Mono (JetBrains Mono / DM Mono) only for numbers inside the fake ledger UI.
- Grain: subtle film noise + faint vignette. Motion: aggressive expo-out eases, hard cuts on the
  beat, 2–4 frame motion-blur smears on fast moves, micro camera shake only in act 1.
- Logo: official A-Star (white version, inline SVG in `/brand`). Two white halves + a star path.
  The star path may glow in `#314DD0` for the reveal; the mark is never stretched, cropped,
  stroked or placed over busy imagery.
